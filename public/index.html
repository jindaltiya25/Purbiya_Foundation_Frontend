About Purbiya Balika Foundation

Organizing Mass Awareness Campaigns In Rural Communities

The Purbiya Prajapati Balika Vivah and Sashaktikaran Foundation was established with the vision of empowering girls through education, awareness, health initiatives, and skill development. We work to create equal opportunities and inspire confidence among young women in rural communities.

Preventing Child Marriages

Conducting awareness campaigns, counseling parents, and encouraging girls to continue their education.

Promoting Girls' Education

Providing educational support, school materials, scholarships, and encouraging enrollment.

Women Empowerment

Organizing vocational training, digital literacy, tailoring programs, and self-employment workshops.
